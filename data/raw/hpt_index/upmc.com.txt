*********************************

For access to the price transparency files, copy this source-page-url and paste it into your internet browser:    
https://www.upmc.com/patients-visitors/paying-bill/pricing-transparency

You will land on the UPMC CMS Pricing Transparency Machine Readable Files page. 
See the Machine Readable Files.  
Click the link associated with location of your choice to download a copy of the file.
The downloaded file will appear in the upper right corner.  
Open that file.
